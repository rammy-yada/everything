YouTube Download Guide

Step-by-step instructions to download YouTube videos and audio

1 Copy the URL
Go to the YouTube video or playlist you want to download. Copy the URL from the address bar or tap Share and copy the link.
2 Paste & Fetch Info
Paste the link into the search box above and click Fetch Info. The title, thumbnail, duration, and available formats will appear.
3 Pick Format & Quality
Choose Video (MP4) for video, Audio (MP3) for music, or Best Available for maximum quality. Select a resolution if downloading video.
4 Download
Hit Download and wait for processing. Once ready, a download button appears. Playlists download each item sequentially.
Age-restricted or "Sign in" errors? YouTube may block requests that lack authentication. Click the cookie icon in the top bar to set up cookies from your browser — this lets the downloader access videos as if you were signed in.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

What sites are supported?
Everything Downloader uses yt-dlp which supports over 1,000 websites. If a site has video or audio content, there's a strong chance it's supported. Try pasting the link!
Can I download entire playlists?
Yes! Simply paste a playlist URL and it will detect all items automatically. You'll be able to download the entire playlist in your chosen format and quality.
What's the difference between Video, Audio, and Best?
Video (MP4) gives you a standard video file. Audio (MP3) extracts just the audio track. Best Available downloads the highest quality format the site offers, which might be in a different container format.
Why did my download fail?
Common reasons: the URL might be private/age-restricted, the site may have changed its format, or ffmpeg might not be installed (needed for merging video+audio and converting to MP3). Make sure ffmpeg is installed on the server.
Is this free to use?
Yes, 100% free and open source. Powered by yt-dlp, an open-source project. No ads, no signups, no limits.
